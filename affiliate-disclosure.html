Which links are affiliate links?

Buttons such as "Check price", "View plans" or "Try … free" on our reviews, and some links inside our articles, are affiliate links. They pass through a short redirect on our site (starting with /go/) so we can count clicks. These links are marked as sponsored for search engines.

No. Ratings are based on our research of official information, user reviews and expert opinion, as described in how we review. We include products we do not earn from when they are relevant, and we publish the cons of every product, including those we earn commissions on.

EDITORIAL POLICY

How we keep our reviews honest

Last updated: November 4, 2026

onapqua. publishes reviews, comparisons and buying guides for software, WordPress plugins, hosting and online services. These standards apply to everything we publish.

Independence

  • Ratings are editorial decisions. Affiliate commissions, sponsorships or free access never change a product's score.
  • We publish weaknesses. Every review lists cons, including for products we earn commissions from.
  • We cover products we don't earn from when they are relevant to readers.

Sponsored content

If a company pays for a review or placement, the article is clearly labelled "Sponsored review" at the top. Sponsors do not approve our verdicts and cannot ask us to remove criticism.

Accuracy

  • We rely on official documentation and pricing pages, then cross-check with independent user and expert reviews. Every review links to its sources.
  • When we describe what users report, it reflects published feedback, not a claim that we ran the same test ourselves.
  • Prices, plans and coupon codes change often. We show when each review was last updated and recheck offers regularly.

Corrections

Found a mistake or an outdated price? Email hello@onapqua.com. We review every report and update the article, changing the "Updated" date when we do.

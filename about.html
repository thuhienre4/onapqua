ABOUT US

Honest reviews for people who buy online tools

onapqua. is an independent review site covering web hosting and servers, WordPress plugins, AI and automation tools, and other online services. Our goal is simple: help you choose faster, avoid bad surprises, and pay a fair price.

How we review

  • Official information first. We read the product's own documentation, feature pages and pricing pages.
  • Real user feedback. We study reviews on Trustpilot, G2, Capterra and community forums, looking for patterns rather than single opinions.
  • Expert reviews. We compare our findings with established tech publications and specialist review sites.
  • Clear scoring. Every review gets a rating out of 5, a list of pros and cons, and links to the sources we used, so you can check our work.

Our reviews are research-based. When a review says what users report, it reflects published feedback from those sources, not a claim that we ran the same test ourselves.

How we score

  • 4.5–5.0 Outstanding: easy to recommend for most people in its category.
  • 4.0–4.4 Very good: a strong choice with minor trade-offs.
  • 3.5–3.9 Good: worth it for specific needs; read the cons.
  • Below 3.5 Mixed: notable issues reported; consider alternatives first.

How we make money

Some links on onapqua. are affiliate links. If you buy through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Commissions never decide a product's rating, and we publish weaknesses even for products we earn from. Read our full affiliate disclosure.

Keeping reviews up to date

Prices, plans and features change often. Each review shows when it was last updated, and we revisit reviews when providers change their offers. Always confirm the final price on the official website before you buy.

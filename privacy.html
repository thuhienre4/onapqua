Privacy policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This policy explains what information onapqua. ("we", "us") handles when you visit the website.

Information we collect

  • Newsletter. If you subscribe, we store your email address to send occasional updates. You can ask us to delete it at any time.
  • Affiliate link clicks. When you click an affiliate link, our redirect records the time, the link and page you came from, the button position, and your device type (mobile, tablet or desktop). We do not store your IP address or browser details. To count unique clicks we use a short code derived from a secret that changes every day, so it cannot be used to recognize you over time. If you rejected cookies or your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we skip even that code.
  • Page views. We count how many times each page is viewed, as anonymous daily totals.
  • Click reference. Some affiliate networks receive a random click reference with your visit so that, if you buy, the network can tell us which article helped. It contains no personal information.
  • Commission reports. Affiliate networks send us transaction IDs, order values, commission amounts and statuses. We never receive your name, payment details or what else you bought.
  • Server logs and security. Like most websites, our hosting infrastructure processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver pages and protect against abuse.

Cookies

  • oq_consent (necessary, 1 year): remembers whether you accepted or rejected analytics cookies.
  • oq_attr (analytics, session only, only if you accept): remembers how you arrived (for example "google / organic" or a campaign name) so we can see which channels lead to affiliate clicks.
  • Google Analytics 4 (_ga, _ga_*; analytics, up to 2 years, only if you accept and GA4 is enabled): measures page views and events such as affiliate clicks, comparisons and newsletter sign-ups. Advertising features are disabled.
  • review_admin (necessary): signs in site administrators only.

We do not use advertising cookies. When you click an affiliate link and visit a provider's website, that provider may set its own cookies to attribute the referral; their privacy policy applies there. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings.

How long we keep data

Click records are kept for about 13 months and then deleted automatically. Commission records are kept as long as needed for accounting.

Third-party services

If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics (Google LLC) processes usage data on our behalf. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and some images are loaded from providers' own websites. These services receive standard request data (such as your IP address) when your browser loads them.

Your choices

You can reject or withdraw analytics consent with Cookie settings; we also honor the Global Privacy Control signal. You can unsubscribe from the newsletter or request deletion of your email by contacting hello@onapqua.com.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

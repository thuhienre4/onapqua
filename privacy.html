Privacy policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy explains what information onapqua. ("we", "us") handles when you visit the website.

Information we collect

  • Newsletter. If you subscribe, we store your email address to send occasional updates. You can ask us to delete it at any time.
  • Affiliate link clicks. We count how many times each affiliate link is clicked. These counts are anonymous totals and are not linked to you.
  • Server logs and security. Like most websites, our hosting infrastructure processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver pages and protect against abuse.

Cookies

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. A session cookie is used only when site administrators sign in. When you click an affiliate link and visit a provider's website, that provider may set its own cookies to attribute the referral; their privacy policy applies there.

Third-party services

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and some images are loaded from providers' own websites. These services receive standard request data (such as your IP address) when your browser loads them.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter or request deletion of your email by contacting hello@onapqua.com.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
